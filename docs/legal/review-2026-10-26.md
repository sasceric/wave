## Implemented

- Localized Terms, privacy, cookies and imprint pages in bs/hr/sr/cnr/sl/en/de,
  linked from the footer and legal-page navigation. Terms address eligibility,
  marketplace contracts, voluntary work, copyright, advertising disclosures,
  moderation, complaints, termination, mandatory consumer rights and changes.
  Wave's own campaigns are explicitly distinguished from its intermediary role.
- Required, unchecked acceptance of the current Terms and an 18+ declaration at
  password/social registration. Missing, false, non-boolean or stale acceptance
  is rejected by the server. Supplied under-18 creator birthdays are rejected.
- Acceptance records identify version, archive hash, locale, source and time.
  Documents are archived in `config/legal/`; existing receipts are not rewritten
  or inferred. Existing users' newsletter choices are preserved, not retroactively
  described as explicit consent.
- Newsletter is an optional unchecked registration choice. Account/profile tips
  remain enabled by default, separate from newsletter and hidden on registration.
  Tips are a bounded setup series, with opt-out, rather than advertising consent.
- Standalone footer newsletter signup starts inactive and sends a confirmation
  link. Opening the page does not confirm: the recipient must explicitly submit
  confirmation. Signed, scoped unsubscribe links and RFC 8058 headers are included
  even in customized signup email layouts. Confirmation/withdrawal are recorded.
  Newsletter withdrawal covers a matching account and standalone subscription;
  resubmission of an email address cannot reverse withdrawal. Admin subscribers
  identify pending, subscribed, withdrawn and legacy records without evidence.
- Privacy purposes/bases, recipients, Cloudflare's global processing, separate
  provider retention, rights/objections, support, messages, push, account deletion
  and backups are described. Shared message text is not falsely promised to be
  erased merely by anonymizing its author's account.
- Cookie controls offer equally prominent accept/reject actions and separate
  analytics/marketing switches. Unversioned, expired (>180 days) or older-version
  choices are not reused. Optional tracking stays off until consent. Google
  Analytics cookies are limited to 180 days without sliding renewal.
- Video galleries no longer request YouTube thumbnails before interaction;
  guide posters are local, and players load after the visitor chooses a video.
- Compiled locale URLs are resolved through `rt()`, avoiding `[object Object]`.

## Schema and rollout

Earlier changes introduced `Version20261026010000` (account legal records) and
`Version20261026120000` (terms notice version). This completion adds
`Version20261026160000` (standalone newsletter records). Columns are nullable;
no historical acceptance is fabricated. Run normal migrations before serving
new code, rebuild the SPA and restart workers. The JSON column migration works
with PostgreSQL and SQLite.

`notifyExistingUsers` remains false. Turning it on sends a one-time informational
Terms email to eligible existing accounts through the existing hourly task.
That email is **not** acceptance. Determine with counsel whether existing
relationships need affirmative acceptance or a notice period before enabling
new contractual provisions for them. Do not fabricate acceptance or block rights
requests because a person has not accepted new Terms.
