# Wave legal/product review — 26 October 2026

Scope: implementation review of the public legal pages, registration, email
preferences and cookie controls. This is a product gap assessment, not a legal
opinion or a certification of compliance. Draft wording and policy decisions below
need review by counsel familiar with the operator and the countries served.

## Implemented in this change

- Registration's optional checkbox describes the newsletter only, starts unchecked,
  and is false when omitted from either password or social registration. Profile
  completion tips stay enabled independently, as requested. Existing account
  preferences are preserved; necessary account/security/collaboration messages
  remain independent of newsletter preferences.
- Privacy notices identify the controller using the existing imprint details and
  explain newsletter choices, the complaint route, the bounded profile-tip series, private chat/link
  previews, push subscriptions and account deletion/shared history in all seven
  languages. The existing account, deletion and email settings implement those
  descriptions.
- Legal-page URL values are resolved with Vue I18n's `rt()` before use. Production
  catalogs contain compiled message objects, which previously became
  `/[object Object]` links. Regression coverage uses compiled catalogs in all seven
  languages for both privacy and cookie pages.
- A compact cookie panel retains independent analytics/marketing permission,
  equally sized accept/reject controls and the existing shared switches. The
  initial view stays small; Customize opens the detailed settings. Reopening from
  the footer opens the detail view with the saved choices. Optional tracking is
  never enabled by opening the panel or changing an unsaved switch.

- Terms of Use page (all seven languages, footer link) with required, versioned
  acceptance at password and social registration (`termsAccepted` + `termsVersion`,
  stored in `wave_user.legal_records`, migration `Version20261026010000`). Creators
  with a birthday under 18 are refused. The terms text is a draft: have counsel
  approve it before deploying, and decide how existing accounts accept it.
- The cookie policy lists every first-party cookie/storage item with its purpose
  and lifetime (session cookie, language, consent record, registration return
  page, service-worker cache) and the optional Google Analytics and Meta cookies.
  Consent saved under an older policy version, or more than 180 days ago, is not
  reused; the panel asks again with optional tracking off.
- Registration tests send the terms fields; new tests cover missing/outdated terms
  and under-18 creators.

- The privacy notice names the providers and retention confirmed by the operator:
  hosting, database, files and backups at Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany), backups
  deleted after 14 days; email delivery by Brevo (Brevo SAS, France); application
  and email logs kept 14 days (`LOG_RETENTION_DAYS`).

- Self-service account deletion sends a confirmation email to the former address
  (`account_deleted`). Existing accounts get a one-time "updated Terms of Use" email
  per terms version (`terms_updated`, hourly `TermsNoticeTask`), but only after
  `notifyExistingUsers` is set to `true` in `config/legal_documents.json` once the
  text is approved. Both are editable in Admin → Email templates. Migration
  `Version20261026120000` adds `wave_user.terms_notice_version`.

## Decisions and documents still needed

### 1. Terms of Use and acceptance records — high priority

The imprint explains the operator and Wave's marketplace role, but is not a
complete agreement. Draft and approve Terms of Use covering:

- Who may open accounts, minimum age, company/agency authority and account approval.
- Wave's matching/messaging role; who contracts with whom; prices, taxes, payment
  outside Wave, cancellation, delivery, acceptance and disputes. Explain that
  voluntary collaborations have no agreed fee and are not a guarantee of free work.
- Any present/future credit pricing, expiry, refunds, paid services and consumer
  withdrawal requirements. Confirm actual commercial rules before publishing them.
- Portfolio/campaign rights: users retain ownership, must hold publication rights,
  and give only the display/hosting permissions Wave needs. Promotion outside the
  platform needs its own defined permission; do not silently claim rights to ads.
- Advertising/sponsorship disclosures, prohibited conduct, reporting, moderation,
  suspensions, appeals, review publication and account termination.
- Changes to terms, contact/complaints and applicable law without overriding
  mandatory consumer protections. Avoid invented liability caps or guarantees.

Add a localized public page, footer/registration links and server-side acceptance
of a specific terms version once this text is approved. A statement that someone
has read a privacy notice does not record acceptance of marketplace terms.

### 2. Privacy purposes, retention and vendors — high priority

The current notice remains too general for a complete processing inventory.
Confirm the lawful basis per purpose, recipients, deployment providers, storage
countries, international-transfer arrangements and actual retention periods.
The notice links to the verified BiH supervisory authority. Relevant features include Brevo email delivery,
Google/Apple sign-in, analytics, Meta Pixel, hosting/CDN, public media, support
attachments, push endpoints, email logs/previews and link-preview requests.

Do not invent vendor contract terms, adequacy decisions or retention promises.
Application/email logs use configurable `LOG_RETENTION_DAYS`; backups, the
provider's own delivery records and shared conversation history need separate,
verified schedules. Explain actual deletion limits, rather than promising to
remove every copy immediately. Publish a revision date and document later changes.

Profile tips are enabled by default as requested, but must stay limited to helpful
account/profile setup instructions. If their content promotes campaigns, brands
or other sales, assess marketing consent separately. Record the legitimate-interest
assessment if that is the chosen basis; an opt-out alone does not establish it.

Newsletter and cookie booleans/settings are not a complete historical consent
ledger. Assess a proportionate record of time, purpose, source and notice version,
plus withdrawal, without collecting unnecessary device/IP data. Existing
subscribers were previously preselected: do not describe historical subscriptions
as proven explicit consent or silently re-subscribe anyone. Plan a consent review
before a bulk promotional send.

### 3. Cookies and embedded media — medium priority

The inventory is published (see above); verify it against production, including
the session cookie name if the server overrides PHP's default `PHPSESSID`. Review whether profile video embeds connect to third parties before a
click/permission; `youtube-nocookie.com` alone does not establish consent compliance.
Keep analytics and marketing choices separate from account/email preferences.

### 4. Operational checks — medium priority

Document rights-request handling and identity checks, processor agreements,
security incidents, moderation/rights complaints, staff access and retention
jobs. Review accessibility and language clarity on the registration/legal pages.
Confirm the imprint's legal name, registration/tax details and contact details.
